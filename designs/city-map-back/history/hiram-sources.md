## Verified

Spec rows

- [T1] The Johnsons: came from Vermont in 1818 (A, C); farm with "productive fields, pasturage, and dairy operations" by 1831 (A). Lyman baptized February 1831 (A, C). John and Elsa read the Book of Mormon with their minister Ezra Booth; traveled to Kirtland; Elsa's rheumatic arm healed when Joseph Smith blessed her; baptized "a short time later" (A, C).
- [T1] The House: "two-story, five-bedroom home was built in 1828, it was one of the finest in the area" (H).
- [T1] Why Joseph Came: sought a "peaceful, secluded place in which to continue his work of translating the Bible" (C). Joseph and Sidney translated in an upstairs room that winter (B). Sidney Rigdon as scribe (D, heading to 71). Bible revision "the impetus for many of the revelations" (A).

What Happened Here

- [T1] Arrival 12 September 1831 with four-month-old adopted twins Joseph and Julia; Sidney and Phebe Rigdon with six young children in a log cabin across the road (C).
- [T1] Four special conferences 1 to 12 November 1831 (D, heading 70); decision to publish the revelations as the Book of Commandments (D, heading 67; A; I). Conference vote that the revelations were "worth to the Church the riches of the whole Earth" (D, heading 70, quoted). Section 1 received November 1, 1831, at the special conference in Hiram as "the Lord's preface" (D, heading 1).
- [T1] December 1, 1831: translation "temporarily laid aside" so Joseph and Sidney could preach, to allay feelings stirred by letters published by Ezra Booth (D, heading 71; B says the letters ran in a local newspaper). January 10, 1832: continue to translate (D, heading and summary of 73).
- [T1] February 16, 1832: Joseph, Sidney "and about twelve other men" in an upstairs room (B); vision while translating John 5:29 (D, heading 76); resurrection of the just and unjust (B).
- [T1] March 24, 1832 (Saturday): "a mob composed of members of the local militia broke into the Johnson home and tore Joseph from his bed"; carried to a meadow some distance away; he and Sidney Rigdon tarred and feathered; Emma nursed and cleaned him; next day he preached at the regular Sabbath service and baptized three (A). Emma scraped tar and Elsa Johnson helped through the night (B). Twins had measles; little Joseph died "later that week" (B).
- [T1] Smiths moved back to Kirtland 12 September 1832, "exactly one year from the day they first arrived" (C). Spring 1834: John Johnson sold most of his land, donated $3,000 to the Church; family moved to Kirtland and "took over the operation of an inn near the Newell K. Whitney store" (C).

On the Map

- [T1] Address 6203 Pioneer Trail (A, H). Joseph preached from the front steps/stoop to members in the yard (G, C). Restored to its original state (H); dedicated by President Gordon B. Hinckley October 28, 2001 (G).
- [T1] Hiram Ward meetinghouse "lies only an alfalfa field to the west of the Johnson farm"; the 2001 dedicatory program was held there (G).

Trivia

- [T1] Some elders questioned the "imperfections" in the language of the revelations; the revelation (D&C 67) challenged the elders to write one "like unto" those Joseph dictated (F); heading summary: "He challenges the wisest person to duplicate the least of His revelations" (D, 67).
- [T1] Sidney "looked limp and pale"; Joseph smiled and said, "Sidney is not used to it as I am." (B, citing Philo Dibble's narratives).
- [T1] Revelation Book 1: Cowdery and John Whitmer left Hiram in November 1831 with Revelation Book 1, arriving in Independence January 1832 (E; F gives Whitmer's departure as 20 November 1831). Whitmer, Cowdery and Phelps used it as printer's manuscript, "marking up corrections, changes, and verse numbers therein," for the Star and the Book of Commandments; later taken to Ohio, where it "served yet again as a printer's manuscript" for the 1835 Doctrine and Covenants (E). Copy says Phelps was "the printer" (E: appointed church printer by revelation).

## Dropped

- Number of revelations received in Hiram. Church sources disagree: 16 now in the D&C (A, G, H), 17 sections (C), 15 (J, 1994). Copy gives no count. Evan may expect "16 revelations"; it is the most repeated figure but not settled.
- Distance from Kirtland: 25 miles (E, JSP place gloss), about 30 (B), 31 (C), 32 (H), 35 (J), 40 (I). Omitted.
- Farm size: 260 acres by 1831 (A) versus "most of his 160 acres" sold in 1834 (C). Not necessarily contradictory but reads that way; copy says "a big farm."
- Year the house was built: 1828 (H, T1). Doctrine and Covenants Central says 1829 (lead only). Used 1828.
- Graphic details of the attack (choking, acid bottle, attempted mutilation, scratching, tar forced into the mouth, Sidney dragged by the heels) (B). Verified, left out under the sensitivity rule.
- Motives and named suspects for the attack (Simonds Ryder, Nancy Marinda Johnson rumor, Booth's letters stirring neighbors). Leads only or speculative; no blame beyond A's "members of the local militia." The brickyard and kiln where tar was heated (B) also left out.
- Joseph's quote "With my flesh all scarified and defaced" (A). Verified; left out as graphic.
- Elsa Johnson using lard to loosen the tar (B). Verified; left out so trivia stays clear of the attack.
- Joseph blaming the baby's death on cold air from the open door (B). Verified but interpretive; copy states only the death.
- 1956 purchase date of the farm. Search summary only, not on a fetched page. J says the Church bought the home "and several hundred surrounding acres" before the 1969 dedication by Elder Mark E. Petersen on May 5, 1969; left out for length.
- Johnson family members leaving the Church in 1837 to 1838 and Luke Johnson's return (C). Verified; outside the Hiram frame of time and not needed.
- Five early apostles with Johnson ties; Lorenzo and Eliza R. Snow hearing Joseph preach on the stoop (C). Verified; cut for length. Mantua is outside the frame.
- D&C 133 added on November 3 as "the Appendix" (D, headings 69, 133). Verified; cut for length.
- Book of Commandments print run (10,000 planned, cut to 3,000; D, heading 67). Verified; cut in favor of the Revelation Book 1 fact.
- Revelation Book 1 page-numbering slip (two pages numbered 134 and two 135) and the 2024 return of loose leaves to the Church (E). Verified; cut for length.
- Rigdon cabin as a separate On the Map item. Location of the cabin today not found in a fetched source; folded into the arrival bullet.
