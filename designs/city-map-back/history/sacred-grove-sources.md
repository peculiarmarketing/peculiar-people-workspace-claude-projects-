## Verified

Spec rows

- [T1] A Farm Out of Forest: farm two miles south of Palmyra (B); first payment on a 100-acre lot of densely forested land (A, B); about 30 acres cleared by 1820 (A, C, E); woodlands left on the farm's eastern and western sides (E).
- [T1] Two Houses: log home one and a half stories, two rooms down and two up, foundation 18 by 30 feet (C); completed by fall 1818 (B), family of 10 moved in winter 1818 to 1819 (A). Copy: "finished in 1818 and soon holding ten people." Frame home about twice the size (A); begun by Alvin before his death in November 1823 (A, B, C); "Alvin never lived to see the house completed" (D). Family in the frame home 1825 to 1829, then back in the log home (C; D: moved back in 1829).
- [T1] The Exact Spot: "The exact location of ... even the precise site of the Prophet's first vision are unknown" (B). Vision "somewhere on the still-wooded part of the farm" (A). The 20-acre old-growth area on the western boundaries of the historic site "referred to as the Sacred Grove since as early as 1905" (E).

What Happened Here

- [T1] Spring 1820, Joseph 14 (E, H); "early in the spring" (F 1:14); first attempt to pray vocally (F 1:14); probably near an area being cleared (A). Copy frames the appearance as Joseph's account.
- [T1] September 21, 1823: harvest under way; Moroni appeared in a bedroom of the log home Joseph shared with others, three separate visits that night (B); "as he and his brothers slept in the log home" (A); after the third visit day was approaching (F 1:47).
- [T1] September 1827: Joseph and Emma living in the frame home when he retrieved the plates (A, B, C). Plates under the hearth bricks, then the cooper's shop loft (B, C). Empty chest buried under the floorboards; mob tore up the floor and smashed the chest but did not search the loft above (D).
- [T1] 1907: Joseph F. Smith visited in 1905; Church purchased the farm two years later; maintained as a working farm until the 1980s (A). K: Sacred Grove acquired 1907.
- [T1] March 27, 1998: Hinckley's dedicatory prayer for the log home replica on its original site (G). Logs: Church hired Robert Parrott in 1997 "to harvest timber from a nearby section of forest to reconstruct" the log cabin (J); I (Parrot) also mentions getting the logs out.

On the Map (frame lat 43.0035 to 43.0759, lon -77.2831 to -77.2094; OSM coordinates; all inside)

- Joseph Smith Log Home, Stafford Road, 43.03998, -77.24095. Inside. [T1] Replica on Stafford Road (G caption); reconstructed on the foundations of the original (K).
- Joseph Smith Frame Home, 43.03818, -77.24056; Cooper Shop 43.03830, -77.24109. Inside. [T1] Frame home about 125 yards south of the log home (C). Log home, frame home, cooper shop and barn restored (D). Barn not in OSM; on the farm (D).
- Sacred Grove (OSM wood polygon), 43.03971, -77.24625. Inside. [T1] West across the farm (H); trails (E).
- Palmyra New York Temple, 43.03900, -77.23706 (matches the brief's halo point 43.0390, -77.2371). Inside. [T1] Dedicated April 6, 2000 by President Hinckley (D, H). Built on a drumlin on the eastern side of the Smith family farm; rock hedge built by Joseph and his brothers lines the grounds to the north and east; youth project "rebuilt the rock wall that was originally built by the Smith family after construction crews moved rocks" (H). [T2] L: east end of the original 100-acre farm.

Trivia

- [T1] "Up to 1,500 maple trees, from which they gathered sap in the spring and converted it into molasses and sugar" (D). A also mentions sap from numerous maple trees for early income.
- [RSC, single academic source] Six trees "approximately 225 years old," one "probably between 300 and 350"; called witness trees (I, 2010). Earlier management cleaned out rotting tree bases and filled them with cement (Porter's 1968 recollection, I). Downed logs now left on the forest floor; wildflowers, woodpeckers, wild turkeys returned (I). Program dates from 1998: Environmental Design and Research study and hiring of Parrot (I); [T1] J: Church asked Parrott in 1998 to implement a new forestry management plan; previously kept "much like a park."

## Volatile (re-check before reprint)

- Witness-tree ages are as of 2010. Trees die; an ice storm in 2003 took over a hundred trees (I). Re-check the count before reprint.
- "Restored" status of the barn and cooper's shop as of 2001 (D).

## Open doubt

- The witness-tree figures rest on one source (I). It is a BYU Religious Studies Center publication co-authored by the Church's senior curator and the grove's forester, so it is strong, but it is not Tier 1. The Church News obituary (J) confirms the 1998 program, not the tree ages. If Evan wants strict Tier 1 only, swap the last trivia for the maple-tree item and move something else up.

## Dropped

- Year of the land purchase. B: initial payment fall 1817; A: "after saving for two years"; the Church History Topics page "Palmyra and Manchester" (https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/topics/palmyra-and-manchester) says the family "purchased" the farm in 1820; E says moved to the property in 1818. Copy gives no purchase year.
- Date the Smiths lost title. B and C: ownership transferred to Lemuel Durfee December 20, 1825; A: lost title in 1826. Two Church sources disagree; the loss is left out of copy entirely.
- Grove acreage. E: 20 acres of old growth; I (2010): about 7 acres originally, then 13, regenerating toward 150. Left out.
- Willard and Rebecca Bean as farm managers (A). Their Cumorah work is in `manchester`.
- Alvin's death detail (calomel, gangrene, B). Not needed; avoids medical detail.
- Plates hidden in bed between Catherine and Sophronia (D, citing a granddaughter's transcript in RLDS Archives). Family tradition one generation removed; omitted.
- The Eight Witnesses on the farm, summer 1829 (C). True, cut for length; Fayette has the Three Witnesses context.
- 116 lost pages news received at the frame home in 1828 (A). Cut for length.
- Temple stained glass (17,000 pieces, H). Temple-product material, not Church history.
- Hinckley's dedicatory prayer and remarks (G, D, H). Reverent; left out per the trivia rule.
- Parrott's self-description as a "scruffy gentile" (J). True and light, but cut to keep the trivia on nature.
- "Alvin tree" (Deseret News only). Lead only; omitted.
- First Vision theology beyond Joseph's account. Out of scope.
