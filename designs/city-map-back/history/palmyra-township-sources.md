Internal. Never goes on the site. This section merges the two dropped sections. No new research: every sentence is carried over from one of them, so every fact, source, tier and dropped claim is in:

The Sacred Grove section's witness-tree trivia was left out (its only source is a BYU article, not a Church page); the maple trivia from the Liahona is used instead.
