# Palmyra township: source ledger

Internal. Never goes on the site. Rebuilt 9 October 2026 after Evan's review: he dropped the Palmyra village and Sacred Grove maps and had this map widened to cover both (centre 43.0737, -77.2066, 12.5 km; frame lat 42.9983 to 43.1491, lon -77.2834 to -77.1298). This section merges the two dropped sections. No new research: every sentence is carried over from one of them, so every fact, source, tier and dropped claim is in:

- `palmyra-village-sources.md`: Canal Town, The First Edition, the 1817, June 1829, August 25 1829 and March 26 1830 entries, the Grandin building, the Erie Canal, Alvin Smith's grave, the loose sheets and mortgage trivia.
- `sacred-grove-sources.md`: A Farm Out of Forest, the spring 1820, September 1823 and September 1827 entries, the Smith farm (1907 purchase, 1998 log home, frame house, barn and cooper's shop), the Sacred Grove (exact spot unknown, name since 1905), the temple (April 6, 2000; rock wall), the maple trivia.

Changes in merging: the village section's "The Printer" row, the autumn 1829 Hyrum Smith entry and the January 1830 Abner Cole entry were cut for length (still verified in the village ledger). Grandin's name moved into The First Edition row. The Sacred Grove section's witness-tree trivia was left out (its only source is a BYU article, not a Church page); the maple trivia from the Liahona is used instead.

Frame check: both old frames (village: lat 43.0419 to 43.0781, lon -77.2485 to -77.2116; Sacred Grove: lat 43.0035 to 43.0759, lon -77.2831 to -77.2094) lie wholly inside the new frame, so every landmark their ledgers placed in frame is inside this one.

## Markers (9 Oct 2026)

Frame lat 42.9983 to 43.1491, lon -77.2835 to -77.1297; merge distance 324 m; temple at 43.0389988, -77.2370607. All seven markers checked inside the frame. Distances are marker to marker (flat-earth metres at this latitude). On the Map is now a numbered list in story order.

1. Erie Canal: 43.06598, -77.23397 (OSM node 4143036289, "Erie Canal", on the Erie Canalway Trail in the village). Exact (the canal itself; present-day map fact, see palmyra-village ledger). Story order: the Canal Town row, 1816 to 1825. 363 m from marker 5.
2. Smith farm: log home, frame house, barn, cooper's shop. Marker at the log home, 43.03998, -77.24095 (OSM). Exact: the log home was rebuilt on its original foundation (sacred-grove ledger K, G), the frame house is original and restored (D). Merge: frame house 43.03818, -77.24056 is 202 m from the log home, so one entry. The frame house is also 298 m from the temple point, under 324 m, which is why the marker sits at the log home (334 m from the temple), not at the frame house. Story order: 1818.
3. Sacred Grove: 43.03971, -77.24625 (OSM wood polygon centroid). Traditional: the grove is named "since as early as 1905" (sacred-grove ledger E; re-read on the Church location page https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/locations/sacred-grove?lang=eng, fetched 9 Oct), and B says "the precise site of the Prophet's first vision [is] unknown"; A, "somewhere on the still-wooded part of the farm." Copy says the marker stands for the grove, not a known spot. 431 m from marker 2, 748 m from the temple. The Church page pin (43.04054, -77.24687) is 100 m from the OSM centroid; OSM used because its polygon is the wood itself (see the Alvin pin problem below).
4. Hill Cumorah: marker at the Angel Moroni Monument, 43.00651, -77.22425 (OSM). Exact (the monument). Merge: visitors' center 43.00698, -77.22578 is 134 m away. Decision to mark it here: the section's 1823 and 1827 bullets both depend on the hill (Joseph went there the day after Moroni's visits; the plates he hid at the frame house came from it), and the hill is the largest landmark inside this frame. It stays Manchester's story: the entry is short, no purchase or pageant history. Facts reused from manchester ledger: plates found September 22, 1823, obtained September 22, 1827 (F, D; re-read on https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/locations/hill-cumorah?lang=eng, fetched 9 Oct); "on the west side of this hill, not far from the top" (D, JS-H 1:51); hill in Manchester (F); about three miles south of the farm (L, O); monument dedicated 1935 (G, I, J). Certainty note in copy: no source places the plates closer than Joseph's description. Story order: 1823.
5. Grandin building and Alvin Smith's grave. Marker at the Grandin building, 43.06386, -77.23056 (OSM; 217 East Main per Church page). Exact. Merge: Alvin's grave, Swift Burying Ground, OSM node 357648976 at 43.06490, -77.23400, is 302 m away, under 324 m, so it joins this entry and is not marked on its own. The grave point is 120 m from the canal marker, but it carries no marker. Coordinate check: the Church location page pin for the gravesite (43.06473, -77.23651) is 247 m west of Church Street on the TIGER/Line road file (Church St runs lon -77.2334 to -77.2337); OSM's node is 47 m from Church Street, so OSM is used and the Church pin is treated as a geocoding offset. Story order: by the main place, June 1829.
6. Martin Harris farm: 43.08354, -77.23972 (OSM building way 829027355 at 2095 Maple Avenue; the Church location page pin is 43.08368, -77.23973, 15 m away). Exact for the farmhouse site. New facts, [T1] Church location page "Martin and Lucy Harris Farm," https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/locations/martin-harris-farm?lang=eng (fetched 9 Oct): address 2095 Maple Ave, Palmyra; mortgaged in 1829 to Grandin; "In 1937, 88 acres of the original Martin Harris farm including the cobblestone house where the original farmhouse once stood, were purchased by Willard Bean on behalf of" the Church. North of the village: JSP township page (palmyra-village ledger G). Not used: the page's "320-acre farm" and "He later sold 151 acres ... to pay off the debt" (conflicts with the JSP account in the village ledger, where the property sale paid Rogers; acreage left out). Story order: August 25, 1829.
7. Palmyra New York Temple: 43.0389988, -77.2370607 (brief), kind temple. Dedicated April 6, 2000 (sacred-grove ledger D, H). Story order: 2000.
