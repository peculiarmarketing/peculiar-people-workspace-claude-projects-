## Verified

Spec rows

- [T1] On Today's Maps: Harmony Township, Susquehanna County, now part of Oakland Township; Harmony village no longer in existence (A). Oakland Township (formerly Harmony) (D). "There is a community in western Pennsylvania near Pittsburgh that is now called Harmony" (G).
- [T1] Emma's Family: born July 10, 1804, in Willingsborough (later Harmony), seventh of nine children of Isaac and Elizabeth Lewis Hale; Isaac shipped meat and other merchandise downriver to Philadelphia and Baltimore (I). Isaac Hale: "Farmer, hunter, innkeeper" (M, in B). Stowell's crew boarded at the Hales' (K ch. 4 via Afton ledger; C: "the crew boarded at the farm of Isaac and Elizabeth Hale"); Emma met Joseph "at the end of October 1825" (I). Copy says "late 1825."
- [T1] Scripture From Here: most of the Book of Mormon translated in Harmony; 15 sections of the Doctrine and Covenants received there (D, E, F; C: "15 revelations now included in the Doctrine and Covenants").

What Happened Here

- [T1] December 1827: JS 1832 history, "in December following we mooved to Susquehana" (quoted in B); C: "They arrived in Harmony in December"; J 1:62 "the month of December." B notes Knight recalled November and Harris late October or early November; copy follows Joseph's own history and C. February 1828: moved onto a 13½-acre lot adjoining the Hales' residential property with a small house; taxed on the house January 3, 1828, "likely moved into the house in February" (B); C: "In February 1828, Joseph and Emma moved into a small home."
- [T1] June 15, 1828: Martin Harris arrived April 12, 1828; "By June 14, at least 116 pages had been translated"; Harris took the manuscript to show his family; first child, a boy, born June 15 and died shortly after; Emma gravely ill (C). Birth the day after Martin's departure; Emma nearly died; Joseph took a stagecoach north; pages lost (K ch. 5). Baby died soon after birth on June 15, 1828 (I). D&C 3: Harmony, July 1828, relating to the loss of 116 pages (L).
- [T1] April 7, 1829: Oliver Cowdery taught school and boarded with Joseph's parents; walked to Harmony with Samuel Smith (K ch. 6: "set out on foot"); arrived April 5; began writing April 7 (J 1:66 to 67; C; B). Six to eight manuscript pages a day (D: "Most of the Book of Mormon translation occurred in Harmony in less than three months, at a rate of six to eight manuscript pages translated per day").
- [T1] May 15, 1829: into the woods to pray about baptism; John the Baptist conferred the Aaronic Priesthood; baptized each other (J 1:68 to 72; K ch. 7: "walked to the river and waded in"; L D&C 13 heading "near Harmony, Pennsylvania, May 15, 1829").
- [T1] August 26, 1830: Hale accepted the June 21, 1830 interest payment and "waited until 26 August 1830 to receive payment in full" (B). Joseph and Emma paid off the farm with help from friends and moved to Fayette (K ch. 9). B: moved to New York "within days"; C: left "around mid-September"; I: "In September 1830, she and Joseph moved to Fayette." Copy: "Soon after." "Emma left the Susquehanna Valley and the Hale family for the last time, never to see her parents and many other relatives again" (I).
- [T1] September 19, 2015: Russell M. Nelson, President of the Quorum of the Twelve, formally dedicated the site (D, F). "After three years of development" (E). Old highway, stemming from a road Isaac Hale helped construct, went between the Hale and Smith homes; the new stretch of State Route 171 was completed by PennDOT "with participation from the Church"; leaders would not proceed until the hazard was relieved (E).

On the Map

- [T1] Reconstructed Smith and Hale homes, "carefully replicated from photographs and research and constructed on the footprint of the original structures, known from the remnant foundation stones, which now comprise the façade of the Hale home's foundation and a fence surrounding the Smith home" (E). Reconstructed homes also in D, F, H.
- [T1] Sugar maple grove ("sugar bush") adjacent to the visitors' center (E); trails to the sugar bush "where the Aaronic priesthood was likely restored" and to the Susquehanna banks where Joseph and Oliver were baptized (H). G quotes JSP historian Spencer McBride: "they most likely went to a grove of sugar maple trees on the property."
- [T1] McKune Cemetery, "where Joseph and Emma Smith buried their first child and where Emma's parents are buried, is adjacent to the Aaronic Priesthood Restoration Monument" (H). Monument erected in 1960, sculpted by Avard Fairbanks, funded by the Aaronic Priesthood quorums (E); "In 1960 the Church placed a monument" (H). Isaac Hale died January 11, 1839; Elizabeth Hale February 16, 1842 (M, in B; not in copy).

Trivia

- [T1] Riverbank picture: "Contrary to long-held misconception, historians are now confident from research that the visitation occurred there, not on the riverbank. An early artistic depiction of the visitation showed it occurring next to the river, and the popular misconception stemmed from that" (E). G (McBride) agrees.
- [T1] April 6, 1829 agreement: Cowdery, "On Monday the 6th, I assisted him in arranging some business of a temporal nature, and on Tuesday the 7th, commenced to write the book of Mormon" (B, quoting Messenger and Advocate, Oct. 1834). The temporal business "included writing this agreement for JS to purchase the property and home from Isaac Hale and serving as witness to it"; $64 handed to Hale that day as the initial payment toward $200; handwriting of Oliver Cowdery; witnessed by Oliver Cowdery and Samuel Smith; transcript opens "This Agreement made and Concluded this 6th day of April Ano Domini one thousand eight hundred and twenty nine" (B). K ch. 6: contract covered "the small frame home ... as well as the barn, farmland, and nearby spring." Church organized April 6, 1830 (L, D&C 21 heading). The coincidence is stated as a date only; no meaning is claimed for it.

## Dropped

- Hale farm size: 130 acres (C) versus 90 acres (I). Two Church sources disagree; omitted.
- Who built the small house: Emma's brother Jesse (C) versus a house "recently vacated by Emma's brother David" (B). Omitted.
- Move date of late 1827: November (Church News 2015 summary of Nelson's talk; Knight) versus December (Joseph's 1832 history; C). Copy follows Joseph's history.
- Final departure: C says January 1831 "never to return to Harmony," but K ch. 9, B and I put the move to Fayette in late summer or September 1830. Copy dates only the final payment and says "Soon after."
- The infant's name. Church News 2021 calls him Alvin; other Church sources do not name him. Left unnamed per the brief's Alvin-free instruction and because the grave marker text was not verified.
- Melchizedek Priesthood restoration. Date unknown and location given only as "between Harmony ... and Colesville ... on the Susquehanna river" (D&C 128:20; F: "the exact location is not known"). Used in the Colesville section's river trivia instead.
- Anthon transcript trip and the barrel of beans (K ch. 5; Snow). Partly New York events; left out for length.
- D&C 25 (revelation to Emma, July 1830, Harmony) and Sally Knight's confirmation with Emma (K ch. 9). Verified, cut for length.
- Moroni returning the plates on September 22, 1828 (C). Verified; considered for the date trivia but it leans on the 1827 Manchester event. Not used.
- Pennsylvania Rep. Sandra Major remarks, the 25-minute film, 1,000th visitor (D, E). Not history.
- Nelson's 2023 book quote ranking Harmony second to Palmyra (G). Reverent tone; not used.

## Markers (9 Oct 2026)

Frame lat 41.9049 to 42.0038, lon -75.6713 to -75.5723; merge distance 213 m; no temple. All three markers checked inside the frame. Coordinates are the OSM points already listed above; no new facts were needed.

1. Smith and Hale homes, McKune Cemetery and the Aaronic Priesthood monument: marker at the Joseph & Emma Homestead, 41.95140, -75.63922 (OSM). Certainty: exact (reconstructions on the original footprints, E; cemetery and monument are the actual sites, H). Merge: Hale home 151 m, monument 50 m and McKune Cemetery 185 m from the Smith home, all under 213 m, so one entry names all of them.
2. Sugar maple grove: 41.95378, -75.63740 (OSM Maple Tree Grove). 304 m from marker 1. Certainty: approximate. H says the restoration was "likely" here; G quotes JSP historian Spencer McBride, "most likely went to a grove of sugar maple trees on the property"; J 1:68 says only "into the woods." Graded approximate rather than traditional because the identification comes from historians' research (E), and the older tradition pointed to the riverbank. Copy says the exact spot isn't known.
3. Susquehanna riverbank (baptism): 41.95063, -75.64212 (OSM, end of the site path at the bank). 255 m from marker 1, 524 m from marker 2. Certainty: approximate. H names the bank where they were baptized; K ch. 7 says they "walked to the river and waded in"; no source fixes the spot along the river. Copy says the marker shows where the trail meets the bank. Note: 217 m from the Hale home, just over the merge distance, but the marker for entry 1 sits at the Smith home (255 m).
