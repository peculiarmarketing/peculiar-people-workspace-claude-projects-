## Verified

Spec rows

- [T1] Across the Line: "Although the farm was located on the Manchester side of the Palmyra-Manchester township line, the Smiths inadvertently built their cabin on the Palmyra side" (A). Log home "situated north of the farm, in Palmyra Township" (B). Joseph Sr. moved "to Manchester, Ontario Co., 1825" (C, person page); family in the frame home 1825 to 1829 (A; Church News 1998 in the sacred-grove ledger). C (place page) also places the First Vision "near border of Palmyra and Manchester townships." OSM puts the Sacred Grove wood polygon in the Town of Manchester and the log home in the Town of Palmyra, consistent with A.
- [T1] The Hill: "glacial drumlin is the largest hill in the region" (E). JS-H 1:51 quote verified verbatim on fetch, "Convenient to the village of Manchester," plates "on the west side of this hill, not far from the top" (D). Three miles: visitors' center "about three miles south of the Sacred Grove" (L); "Three miles to the south is the Hill Cumorah" from the temple (O).

What Happened Here

- [T1] September 22, 1823: plates found in the hill that date (F); "Moroni showed him the gold plates for the first time the following day" (I, J). Lever, stone box "formed by laying stones together in some kind of cement," forbidden to take them, to return in one year (D 1:52 to 53).
- [T1] September 22, 1827: plates obtained (F, I, J); met the angel on the same date each intervening year (F; D 1:54); annual visits 1823 to 1827 (C).
- [T1] 1923 purchase of part of the hill: Church History Topics, Church Historic Sites, lists "the Hill Cumorah in 1923 and 1928" (M). President Heber J. Grant, October 1923 conference, quoted in N: "a farm of ninety odd acres, which embraces the West slope of the Hill Cumorah, about one-third of the way up the hill." Copy: "a farm partway up the west slope."
- [T1] Early 1928: estate lawyer called Willard to his office; Bean bought the hill, three farms bordering it and the Grange Hall for $53,000 (H, which says "February 1928"; N gives Congdon's phone call as January 30, 1928, and the same $53,000 total). Copy says "Early 1928." Sexton "the local banker" (H). Bean's First Presidency assignment to buy historic properties (H). Purchase completed 1928 (I, J, E).
- [T1] 1930s: Willard and Rebecca Bean planted 65,000 evergreens from the New York State Conservation Department and 3,000 seedlings transplanted from the outskirts of the Sacred Grove; top of the hill almost completely bare earlier; farmers had cleared trees for grazing (E).
- [T1] 1935: monument dedicated by Heber J. Grant (G; I and J: "dedicated on the hill in 1935"); sculptor Torleif Knaphus (G).
- [T1] September 21, 2025: Elder Bednar dedicated the refurbished site; "exactly 202 years ago, on September 21, 1823" (I). Reforestation began 2021; 23 pageant buildings removed; about 400,000 square feet (over nine acres) of asphalt, gravel roads, parking and paths removed (I, J).

On the Map (frame lat 42.9299 to 43.0542, lon -77.2524 to -77.1259; OSM coordinates; all inside)

- Angel Moroni Monument, 43.00651, -77.22425. Inside. [T1] Over 30 feet tall; right hand points up, plates in left; granite column with rays of light incised (G).
- Hill Cumorah Visitors' Center, 43.00698, -77.22578 (OSM tags it 659 State Route 21; Church page F lists 603 State Route 21). Inside. Hill Cumorah summit (OSM peak) 42.99451, -77.22026. Inside. [T1] Built in 2002, "recently renovated" (I, J). Trails from the visitors' center to the top (E, G).
- Manchester village, 42.96942, -77.22967. Inside. About 2.8 km (1.7 miles) south of the summit by these coordinates; copy says "about two miles." [T1] Joseph located the hill as "convenient to the village of Manchester" (D 1:51).
- Also inside this frame but left to `sacred-grove`: log home 43.03998, -77.24095; frame home 43.03818, -77.24056; Sacred Grove 43.03971, -77.24625; Palmyra New York Temple 43.0390, -77.2371.

Trivia

- [T1] Manchester formed as Burt Township from Farmington Township, 31 Mar. 1821; renamed Manchester 16 Apr. 1822 (C, citing Conover, French and Child gazetteers).
- [T1] Sexton suggested $100,000; Bean said the Church "had done quite well without the hill for nearly a hundred years and would continue to do so until a more realistic offer was made" (H). N corroborates the $100,000 figure.
- [T1] Myth/mixup correction: the last pageant performed was 2019 (K: "the 2019 performance, the last one conducted"; ran July 11 to 13 and 16 to 20, 2019; L: "the final Hill Cumorah Pageant that took place in 2019"). October 2018 announcement that it would not be held after 2020 (L, K); April 2020 postponement to 2021 (K); cancellation announced March 9, 2021 (K); "canceled and will not be held in future years" (L). Common mixup: many news reports and the Church's own E say the pageant closed "in 2020."

## Volatile (re-check before reprint)

- Visitors' center description ("built in 2002 and has since been renovated"). Stable unless rebuilt.
- Reforestation progress; the copy only states what was removed.

## Dropped

- Pageant start year. Newsroom (I, J) and E say 1935; Church News (K) says it premiered in 1937 and L quotes Elder Christofferson thanking those involved "since 1937." Both are Church sources; copy gives no start year.
- Monument dedication day. G says July 21, 1935; other sources (Church History Library summary via search, not fetched) say July 24. Copy uses the year only.
- Acreage of the 1928 purchase. H: "over six hundred acres"; N: 482 acres added to 97.5 already owned. Left out.
- Grange Hall cost details, Bennett and Tripp farms, the RLDS bidding scare (N). Detail and another church's role; left out.
- George Albert Smith's friendship with Sexton (search summary of a Church history page that did not surface on fetch; E did not contain it). Not verified on a fetched page; omitted.
- Sexton's heirs pledging never to sell (H, N). True, but it reads as blame; left out per the sensitivity rule.
- "Hill Cumorah Historic Site in Palmyra, New York" (I, J). That is the postal address; F and K place the hill in Manchester. Not used as trivia; noted here.
- Gold plates weight and alloy (Church History Topics "Gold Plates," fetched). Verified, hedged in the source ("may have"); not used.
- Book of Mormon geography. F notes the Church "does not take a position" on ancient locations. Kept out entirely.
- Pageant cast and seating figures (770 cast, 8,000 seats, K). True; cut for length.
- Bednar's dedicatory prayer text (I). Reverent; left out per the trivia rule.
- Statue models (Elwin Clark, Don Carlos Clark, G) and "angels attended him" (G). The latter is a subjective account; omitted.
- First Vision and Moroni's night visits in the log home: told in `sacred-grove`; here only "the day after Moroni's visits."
