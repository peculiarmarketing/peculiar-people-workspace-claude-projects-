# Church history sites

Street maps for the places of the Restoration, from Joseph Smith's birthplace to the Salt Lake Valley, plus the first overseas mission. Each row is one map set in `out/<folder>/`. Run a row again with its exact query.

Two kinds of map:

- **Town**: the place's own boundary, as OpenStreetMap draws it. Most rural New York and Vermont "towns" are townships, so the map covers the countryside around the village too.
- **Site, 6 km square**: for places with no town boundary. A 6 km square (3 km radius) around the site, with roads cut at the edge. Run with `--radius 3`.

The one-line notes are for orientation only. Nothing here has been through the fact-check for product copy.
