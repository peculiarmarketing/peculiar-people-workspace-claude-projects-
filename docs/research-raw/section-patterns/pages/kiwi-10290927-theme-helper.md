# How do I enable the Kiwi size chart on my theme?

Add the Kiwi Sizing app block - also called Kiwi Theme Helper - to your product template so the size chart appears on your product pages.

Updated this week

Table of contents

To make sure the Kiwi Size Chart & Recommender (Kiwi) works properly on your store, you need to enable the KiwiSizing app block/embed in your product template.

If you need to reinstall the app, it will automatically restore previously saved settings unless they have been manually deleted, ensuring a seamless reinstallation process.

This step ensures that the size chart appears correctly on your product pages and that all app features function smoothly. For more details, see the article: Shopify Theme Extension Requirement – What It Means for You

## How to Enable the Kiwi App Block / Embed

Follow these steps:

- 

From your Shopify admin, go to Sales Channels > Online Store.

- 

On your published theme, click Customize.

- 

At the top center of the theme editor, open the dropdown and select Products, then Default product.

If your store uses custom product templates, repeat these steps for each one. Adding the block only to the Default product template means the chart appears on some products and not others.

- 

In the left-hand panel, under Product Information, click the + Add block.

- 

Then, go to Apps and select KiwiSizing.

- 

Finally, click Save in the top right corner.

## Why the App Block Matters

Adding the KiwiSizing app block ensures that:

- 

The size chart displays correctly on product pages

- 

All Kiwi app features work smoothly across your store- The size chart remains visible and functional even when customers change product variants such as size or color.

- 

Dynamic variant switching is supported with features like automatic reinjection.

This step is especially important:

- 

Right after installing Kiwi for the first time

- 

After a theme update that may remove or overwrite app blocks

## Important Notes

- 

Make sure to enable the Kiwi Sizing app block on every product template you use.

- 

If your store uses custom product templates, repeat the steps above for each one.- Configure persistent size chart visibility settings in the KiwiSizing app to ensure consistent functionality during customer interactions.

- 

Verify that any custom scripts or workarounds previously implemented do not conflict with automatic reinjection settings.
