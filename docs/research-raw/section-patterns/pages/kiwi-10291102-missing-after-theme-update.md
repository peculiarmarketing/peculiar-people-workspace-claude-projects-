# Restoring Size Charts with the Kiwi App

Size charts are essential for online stores, helping customers choose the right size and make confident purchases. If you notice your size charts are missing after updating your Shopify theme, don’t worry! This is an opportunity to enhance your store by integrating the Kiwi app.

# Here's how to restore your size charts:

- 

Click on "Sales channels," then select "Themes."

- 

Next, click "Customize" to access the theme editor.

- 

In the theme editor, click on "Home page" at the top and switch it to "Products." Then, select "Default products."

- 

Find the "Apps" section in the left panel. Click on "Add block," then search for "Kiwi" to add it to your page. This enables the theme app block/embed.

Once you’ve completed these steps, your size chart will be visible on your live site again. If you need any assistance, feel free to reach out to us!
