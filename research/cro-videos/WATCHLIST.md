## Brand constraints carried into synthesis

Read from BRAND.md before any of this. These decide which findings get thrown out later.

1. Zero orders ever. No reviews, no ratings, no customer photos, no "2,000 happy
   customers." Every piece of social-proof advice on this list assumes an asset you do
   not have. That gap gets its own section, not a quiet drop.
2. Print-on-demand. No volume discount, no control of shipping speed, no inserts today,
   no bundling economics. Free-shipping-threshold and bundle advice mostly does not apply.
3. Every product image is a mockup. Advice to "use real lifestyle photography" is correct
   and currently impossible. It gets flagged as blocked, not as a fix.
4. Premium price with no reviews. Evan is holding the price. Any advice that resolves the
   trust problem with a discount is against a stated brand decision.
5. Navigation is an Easify dropdown, not Shopify collections. Collection-page merchandising
   advice may have nowhere to land.
6. Guardrails: no jokes, no politics, no urgency theatre. Countdown timers, fake stock
   counters, and "17 people are viewing this" are out on brand grounds before they are
   out on ethics grounds.
7. No em dashes anywhere.
