# SPEC: Art Guidelines for DTF and Screen Printed Transfers

- Title: Art Guidelines for DTF and Screen Printed Transfers | How To Get The Best Results
- Channel: Transfer Express
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=08GvH2Z9aUo
- Upload date: 2023-06-20
- Duration: 9:36
- Why this video: the only source in the set with hard minimums for both screen print and DTF (line weight, negative-space gap, positive and knocked-out text) tested on printed shirts, including white ink on a dark (purple) garment.

Readers: one (reader A, transcript plus 160x90 storyboard). Gemini pass did not run, so every line is SINGLE SOURCE (reader A). Note: the screen-print values are for plastisol screen printed heat transfers pressed with a heat press, not direct screen printing.

## Principles

- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Minimums cover two things: positive line weight (including each letter's stem) and show-through (negative space) gaps that must not fill in.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Font stems are lines; "the stem or line of each character no thinner than 1 point" for screen print.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Lines under the minimum can print and look fine but fail durability (screen print 0.5 pt line vs "50-plus wash-dry cycles"; DTF lines under 1.29 pt break up "when we just start rubbing it with our finger").
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Plastisol ink spreads under heat-press pressure; gaps below 2.8 pt fill in, and counters in P and O close into solid ink.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Knocked-out text (garment colour showing through ink) needs to be much larger than positive text; 18 pt is the speaker's smallest safe size, "You might even want to go larger".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): DTF ink "just going straight down" (no spread like plastisol), so DTF holds finer show-through gaps and cleaner knocked-out text than screen print.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): DTF needs a larger minimum line than screen print because the adhesive needs ink plus "a white backing" to hold on to; lines too thin to get white printed behind them lack adhesive and wear off. Visible on the back of the transfer film.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Triage: readable text and important elements must meet minimums; small non-critical details ("a few seeds in a strawberry", "a little corner") may be allowed to drop out.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Safe text size depends on the font; thinner fonts need larger sizes.

## Numbers

- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Screen print minimum line: "1 point" = "0.012 inches", called "the screen printing industry standard".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Font equivalent of a 1 pt stem: "right around 16 points with most fonts when you're printing on fabric".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Positive Arial text on their test: "eight or 9 point font is right where we're clean and clear".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Screen print minimum show-through: "2.8 points or 0.04 inches"; "1.5 point" gap closed up or nearly closed.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Knocked-out text: "18 point font is really the smallest I feel safe going".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): DTF minimum line: "1.29 points".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Test chart ranges: lines "0.5 point to 15 point"; halftone dots "starting at 2 points".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Durability standard: "50-plus wash-dry cycles".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): DTF art help "$26 charge"; screen-print transfer art help free.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Heat press readout digits "362" and "004" visible on screen; what they measure is not said.

## Demonstrated vs said

- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A), shown: the test chart in software; printed screen-print transfers in white on purple and black on white, with fine lines, small text and knocked-out text compared; show-through rows at and below minimum; DTF rows rubbed by finger; back of a DTF transfer showing thin lines without white backing; online "live proof" heat map flagging thin areas.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A), said only: wash failure at 50 cycles (no wash test shown); "16 points with most fonts" (not tested across fonts); pressure-driven spread (no two-pressure comparison).

## Caveats

- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Values are Transfer Express process standards tied to their transfers and a Hotronix press setting.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Positive-text guidance is internally loose: "16 points with most fonts" (general) vs "eight or 9 point" Arial clean on the chart.

## Archetype trap flags

- "1 pt = 0.012 inches" is the video's own figure; arithmetically 1 pt is 1/72 in, about 0.0139 in. Keep the quote, but do not "correct" it silently.
- No DTF minimum show-through value is spoken; any DTF gap number would be imported.
- No DTG guidance here; do not extend DTF numbers to DTG without another source.
- Dot gain percentages and mesh counts are not in this video.
