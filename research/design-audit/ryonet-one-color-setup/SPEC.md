# SPEC: Best Practices for Setting Up a 1-Color Screen Printing Job

- Why this video: chosen as the one-colour screen-print production reference (the seal is one colour); in practice it covers press, platen and workspace setup, with almost nothing the artwork audit can use.

Readers: one (reader A, transcript plus 160x90 storyboard). Gemini pass did not run, so every line is SINGLE SOURCE (reader A).

## Principles

- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Off contact is "the distance between the screen and the [platen]"; set it with "about four cleanup cards", let the screen level naturally, tighten, and check for "an even space".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Keep off contact consistent with a shim ("a washer or quarter") on the front edge of the frame.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): The print head must sit "snugly into the registration gate" each time.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Draw a centre line on the platen to align the shirt with "the center of the design on the screen", plus horizontal lines to place the collar.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Platens too close to the registration gate misalign the screen; multi-station platens must be equidistant from the gate.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Apply platen tape and trim it cleanly; tucked tape snags shirts.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Tape every screen area without emulsion using "specific screen printing tape"; lay it all in one rotational direction so it comes off "in one go".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Keep tools, shirts and flash or conveyor dryer within reach of the press.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Wood platens are cheapest but "prone to warp"; aluminum is thinner and preferred.

## Numbers

- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Off contact "between 1 16 and 1 8 inches" (1/16 in to 1/8 in), "typically".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): "about four cleanup cards" as the spacer.
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Registration lines "one inch two inches and three inches down from the top of the platen".
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Press "Riley Hopkins 150 4x1" (four print heads, one station).
- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Screen tape cut "a little bit longer than the inside length of the screen".

## Caveats

- SINGLE SOURCE (reader A): Off contact range is "typical", not fixed.
