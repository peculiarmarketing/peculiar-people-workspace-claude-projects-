# YouTube: Shorts

Research date 3 September 2026. Split from `youtube-longform.md` because the two are different products with different audiences, different economics and different answers.

## 1. Who is actually there

**United States, platform level.** 84% of US adults use YouTube, the highest of any platform measured (CONFIRMED, Pew, fielded 5 February to 18 June 2025). 48% use it daily. By age: 95% of 18-29s, 92% of 30-49s, 85% of 50-64s, 64% of 65+. By sex: men 86%, women 83%, the most evenly split platform in the study. Meta-style ad reach: 254 million US users, 73% of population (DataReportal, October 2025).

Google does not break out Shorts viewership by demographic. **The Shorts audience specifically is UNKNOWN.** Every number above is YouTube as a whole.

## 2. How a post reaches someone who does not follow the account

Interest media, the same as Reels and TikTok. Shorts are served from a recommendation feed, not a subscription feed.

## 3. Shelf life

Days. Shorts do not accumulate search traffic the way long-form videos do. This is the main thing that separates them from the rest of YouTube.

## 4. Searchability

Weak relative to long form. Shorts appear in search but rank below long-form results for informational queries. UNKNOWN in measurable terms.

## 5. Link behavior

Shorts can link to a related long-form video, which practitioners use as a funnel into the channel (Katie Steckly, 15 September 2025). Outbound links to a store are restricted and not a realistic traffic route.

## 6. Native commerce

YouTube Shopping affiliate programme exists and connects to Shopify, but eligibility requires YouTube Partner Programme membership, which requires either 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers plus 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days (CONFIRMED, support.google.com, current). From 1 February 2027 new applicants will need 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days, or 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days (CONFIRMED, YouTube Help).

**Ten million Shorts views in 90 days is not a threshold this brand will reach, and the bar doubles in five months.** Native commerce on Shorts is out of reach.

## 7. What it demands, and can Evan meet it

9:16 vertical, under 60 seconds, and a specific content style. The most useful finding in this study on Shorts comes from a six-month practitioner test: B-roll plus trending audio plus text, the classic Instagram reel formula, performs well on Instagram, decently on TikTok and **poorly on YouTube Shorts**. "YouTube Shorts viewers prefer a more YouTube style of short form content", meaning original, tutorial, talking head or explanatory content rather than trend-dependent content (Katie Steckly, 15 September 2025, 51,850 views). SINGLE SOURCE but from a controlled six-month test.

**Partly met, and in an interesting way.** Temple Study is explanatory content. It is closer to what Shorts rewards than to what Instagram rewards. The Inspection is closer to Instagram's register. So the same two shows split differently across platforms than intuition suggests.

Carousels cannot go to Shorts at all. That removes three of the Posting Rota's five weekly slots.

## 8. What it is bad at

- **The monetisation door is shut and getting further away.** See section 6.
- **No shelf life and no search compounding**, which forfeits the one advantage YouTube has over every other platform.
- **Carousels do not exist here**, so most of the Rota's output cannot be reused.
- **It competes against the Church's own channels.** See `youtube-longform.md` section 11.
- **It is the wrong half of YouTube for this brand.** Everything YouTube is genuinely good at (search memory, authority, evergreen compounding) lives in long form, and Shorts has none of it.

## 9. Real cost per week

**Ten minutes a week if it receives the vertical video already produced for Instagram and TikTok.** The marginal cost of a third upload of an existing file is small. As a standalone effort it is not worth costing, because the standalone case does not exist.

Going quiet for a month: harmless.

## 10. Rules and account risk

Religious content is unrestricted. Shorts views are ineligible for revenue sharing if the Short is non-original, artificially inflated, or inconsistent with advertiser-friendly guidelines (support.google.com, current). Non-original is the relevant clause and it does not bite here.

## 11. Is there a Latter-day Saint audience here today

Yes, and it is large, but it is watching long-form. See `youtube-longform.md` section 11 for the full evidence. I did not find Shorts-specific Latter-day Saint temple content at scale, and YouTube search results do not distinguish cleanly enough logged out to claim one way or the other. UNKNOWN for Shorts specifically.
