# Threads

Research date 3 September 2026.

## 1. Who is actually there

**United States.** 8% of US adults use Threads (CONFIRMED, Pew, fielded 5 February to 18 June 2025; this was the first year Pew asked). Meta's ad tools reported 24.6 million US users, 8.9% of adults, October 2025 (DataReportal). **Two independent sources within one percentage point. CONFIRMED**, and one of the very few places where survey and ad-reach data agree, which makes this one of the more reliable figures in the study.

**Age, US, June 2025 (Pew, CONFIRMED).** 18-29: 15%. 30-49: 10%. 50-64: 6%. 65+: 3%.

**Sex, US, June 2025 (Pew, CONFIRMED).** Men 8%, women 9%. The most evenly split platform here. Meta's ad tools agree: 50.4% female.

**Global.** Threads "crossed 500 million monthly actives" per Meta's Q2 2026 earnings call. SINGLE SOURCE, company statement, and it is not in the SEC filing. Note the gap between 500 million global and 24.6 million US: Threads is far more of an international product than a US one.

Read against BRAND.md section 10: 8% of US adults is the smallest addressable audience of any platform profiled here except Bluesky and Truth Social. In absolute terms roughly 21 million US adults, before any filtering for interest in temples.

## 2. How a post reaches someone who does not follow the account

Interest media, and aggressively so. Threads' feed is heavily recommendation-driven, more than Instagram's, and new accounts routinely reach strangers. Metricool's 2026 study found Threads averaging 1,536 impressions per post and roughly 25 interactions per post. SINGLE SOURCE, vendor data, global rather than US.

Threads uses a one-tag-per-post system with no hash marks, where "the tag view is the search view" (Adam Mosseri, Threads). SINGLE SOURCE.

Of everything in this study, Threads is arguably the easiest place for a zero-follower account to be seen. It is also the smallest.

## 3. Shelf life

Hours to a day. Threads is a real-time conversational feed.

## 4. Searchability

In-platform search exists and has improved, including filters by profile and date range (Threads official account). Whether public Threads posts are indexed by Google is genuinely muddy: Meta reportedly began allowing Google indexing of public posts in July 2025, controlled through an Instagram setting called "Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results". **Every source I found for that is a Threads user post rather than a Meta announcement. SINGLE SOURCE at best, and I would call it UNKNOWN.**

## 5. Link behavior

Links are clickable in a post, which is more than Instagram allows. Whether Threads downranks link posts is contested. A widely shared claim on Threads itself is that "Threads doesn't downrank links but since people rarely click them" engagement suffers anyway. That is a user post, not a Meta statement. **UNKNOWN.**

Traffic volume available at 8% US penetration is small regardless of how links are treated.

## 6. Native commerce

None. No shop, no checkout, no affiliate programme.

## 7. What it demands, and can Evan meet it

Short text, conversational, replies, personality. It is closer to a group chat than a publishing surface. Images are supported but text carries the platform.

**Partly met, and better than it first appears.** Evan's strongest asset is written and factual. A single well-sourced sentence about a specific temple is a native Threads post in a way it is not a native TikTok post. The Temple Study research library splinters here more cheaply than anywhere except Pinterest.

What does not fit is the conversational, personality-led register, which collides with the unresolved voice and attribution question in BRAND.md section 19.

## 8. What it is bad at

- **It is small.** 8% of US adults. The addressable pool before interest filtering is roughly a twelfth of Facebook's.
- **Hours of shelf life and no reliable search memory.**
- **No commerce and negligible referral traffic.**
- **It is text-first, and the brand's product is visual.**
- **Its behaviour is under-documented.** Link handling, Google indexing and reach mechanics are all UNKNOWN or single-sourced, which makes it hard to plan around.
- **It is gated to logged-out visitors**, so it fails the credibility test: a buyer who finds a Threads profile in search gets a login wall, which I verified.
- **Meta has changed Threads repeatedly**, and a small platform in flux is a poor place to build a habit.

## 9. Real cost per week

**Ten to fifteen minutes a week** if it receives single-fact posts splintered from the Temple Study pillar, which the Posting Rota already produces. As a standalone effort, two hours a week and not worth it.

Going quiet for a month costs nothing.

## 10. Rules and account risk

Threads runs on Instagram's account infrastructure and Meta's Community Standards. Religious content is unrestricted. Meta's originality rules apply. No elevated risk identified.

## 11. Is there a Latter-day Saint audience here today
