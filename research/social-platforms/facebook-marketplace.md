# Facebook: Marketplace

Research date 3 September 2026.

## 1. Who is actually there

Same platform demographics as `facebook-page.md`: 71% of US adults, 52% of US adults daily, peaking at 80% among 30-49 year olds and 78% among women (Pew, fielded 5 February to 18 June 2025). Meta does not publish a separate Marketplace user figure. UNKNOWN.

Marketplace behaviour is different from Facebook behaviour. It is a local, price-led, secondhand-first buying surface. There is no published data on its composition. UNKNOWN.

## 2. How a post reaches someone who does not follow the account

Through Marketplace search and category browse, plus local geography. Follower count is irrelevant. In that narrow sense it is search media.

## 3. Shelf life

A listing lives until it is sold or expires, typically weeks. Listings can be renewed.

## 4. Searchability

Inside Marketplace, yes. Marketplace listings are not meaningfully indexed by Google. A stranger searching "Manti temple shirt" on the open web will not find a Marketplace listing.

## 5. Link behavior

**None. This is the point that disqualifies it.** Marketplace is a destination, not a referral channel. It does not send anyone to peculiarpeopleco.com. Meta's Facebook Marketplace Seller Protection Policy states that "sales must be processed through onsite checkout" to receive seller protections (facebook.com/legal/merchant_policies, current).

## 6. Native commerce

This is all Marketplace is. And the fulfilment obligations are the problem.

Meta's Commerce Product Seller Agreement and shipping terms require, for shipped items (CONFIRMED, facebook.com/legal, current):

- Shipped items must use pre-paid shipping labels issued by Meta.
- Sellers must provide valid tracking for all orders and **ship within 7 calendar days or the order may be automatically cancelled by Meta**.
- When using a shipping label, the seller must get the product to the carrier **within 3 business days**, either by scheduling a pickup or dropping it off.
- Shipping cost is estimated from the package weight declared on the listing, and the seller pays any shortfall if actual weight exceeds it.
- "Failure to maintain adequate shipping performance, including frequent cancellations, late shipments, or failure to deliver items to the carrier, may be considered a violation."

**Peculiar People cannot meet those terms.** The business is print on demand through Printify (BRAND.md section 6). Evan never handles the goods, never sees the printing, and cannot put a package into a carrier's hands within three business days using a Meta-issued label, because the item does not exist until it is printed by a third party who then ships it directly. The obligations assume a seller with inventory in a cupboard.

Meta's Commerce Policies do not separately prohibit print on demand or dropshipping. I checked. The blocker is not a category ban, it is the shipping mechanics.

## 7. What it demands, and can Evan meet it

Photographs of the actual item, a price, a location, and the fulfilment terms above. **No.** Every current store image is a mockup (Playbook readiness board), the garment range is unsettled (BRAND.md section 7), and the fulfilment model is structurally incompatible.

## 8. What it is bad at

- It cannot send traffic to the store.
- Its fulfilment terms are incompatible with print on demand.
- It is a price-led surface, and BRAND.md section 5 holds a deliberately premium price with a stated worry that it is already a barrier. Marketplace is where people go to pay less.
- It builds no brand, no audience and no content asset.
- It is local by design, which fights the beachhead strategy of selling a specific temple to people scattered across its diaspora.
- There is no reverent presentation of a sacred building available on a surface built for used sofas.

## 9. Real cost per week

Not applicable. Recommendation is not to use it.

## 10. Rules and account risk

The seller performance rules above are the risk, and they are the reason to stay off. Repeated late shipment or cancellation is a policy violation that can restrict shipping features on the account.

## 11. Is there a Latter-day Saint audience here today

Not measurable and not investigated. Marketplace search results are personalised by location and I did not run them, having established that the channel is disqualified on fulfilment grounds before audience became relevant. UNKNOWN.

## Verdict

Skip. This is the clearest disqualification in the study, and it is disqualified by a mechanism rather than a judgement call: Meta requires the seller to hand a package to a carrier within three business days using a Meta-issued label, and a print-on-demand seller cannot do that.
