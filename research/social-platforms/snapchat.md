# Snapchat

Research date 3 September 2026. Snapchat has never been assessed for this brand. This is the first pass.

## 1. Who is actually there

**United States.** 25% of US adults use Snapchat, down from 27% in both 2023 and 2024 (CONFIRMED, Pew, fielded 5 February to 18 June 2025). Snapchat's ad tools reported 104 million US users, 33.1% of adults, October 2025 (DataReportal). CONFLICT, resolved toward Pew.

**Age, US, June 2025 (Pew, CONFIRMED).** 18-29: 58%. 30-49: 31%. 50-64: **13%**. 65+: **4%**. This is the youngest skew of any platform in the study apart from nothing, and the collapse after 30 is severe.

**Sex, US, June 2025 (Pew, CONFIRMED).** Women 28%, men 22%.

**Company-reported, quarter ended 30 June 2026 (CONFIRMED, Snap Inc. Q2 2026 earnings release, SEC filing 3 August 2026).**

| Region | Daily active users | Year over year | ARPU |
|---|---|---|---|
| North America | **92 million** | **down 7%** | $10.26, up 23% |
| Europe | 98 million | down 2% | $3.62, up 36% |
| Rest of world | 303 million | up 12% | $1.00, up 4% |
| Global | 493 million | up 5% | |

North America was 92 million in Q1 2026 too. **Flat quarter on quarter, shrinking 7% year on year.** Snap is growing globally by growing everywhere except the market Peculiar People sells to.

That single table is the most decisive fact in this file.

## 2. How a post reaches someone who does not follow the account

Through Spotlight, which is Snapchat's interest-media surface, and through Discover. Snap states over 350 million Snapchatters engage with Spotlight monthly (forbusiness.snapchat.com, SINGLE SOURCE, company figure, global not US). Public Profiles are "instantly discoverable throughout Snapchat" via Search, mentions, Discover, Lens Explorer and ads (Snapchat for Business, current).

So yes, mechanically it is interest media. A brand with no audience can reach strangers. That part is real.

## 3. Shelf life

**The shortest in the study.** Snapchat's whole design premise is ephemerality. Stories expire. Spotlight videos persist longer but do not accumulate. Saved Highlights sit on a profile that almost nobody visits.

## 4. Searchability

Snapchat has a search function and a public web surface at `snapchat.com/explore/<query>`, which is more than X, LinkedIn or Threads offer a logged-out visitor. Google indexes some of it.

But searchability is only worth something if the search returns the right things, and for this brand it does not. See section 11.

## 5. Link behavior

Public Profiles can carry a website link and a product catalogue link. Links can be attached to Snaps and Stories. This is better than Instagram on paper. In practice the traffic volume available is a function of the audience size, which for this niche is the problem.

## 6. Native commerce

Public Profiles support a product catalogue and a store link. There is a Snapchat monetisation programme for creators. There is no creator commission marketplace comparable to TikTok Shop or Facebook's new affiliate tools. Snapchat is not a commerce platform in any meaningful sense for a brand like this.

## 7. What it demands, and can Evan meet it

Vertical video, casual, fast, personal, and typically a face. Snapchat's register is private communication between friends, and content that reads as produced is out of place.

**Poorly met.** The brand is "premium, reverent, understated" (BRAND.md section 5). Snapchat is none of those things. The Posting Rota's assets would work mechanically, in that a 9:16 file uploads, but they would be wrong for the room. And neither operator should be assumed willing to appear on camera.

## 8. What it is bad at

- **Its US audience is shrinking.** Down 7% year over year in North America, flat quarter on quarter. Every other platform in this study is flat or growing in the US.
- **It skews far too young.** 13% of 50-64s and 4% of 65+. Two of BRAND.md's four avatars are effectively absent.
- **Zero shelf life**, which forfeits the compounding that makes Pinterest and YouTube worth the effort.
- **No search memory for this subject.** See section 11.
- **No commerce mechanism worth using.**
- **The tone is wrong for the brand**, and that is not a fixable problem.
- **It is a private-messaging app first.** Most usage is one-to-one communication, not content consumption. A brand is a guest in a room built for friends.
- **Nothing it does is unique.** This was the question the round had to answer, and the answer is that Snapchat's one genuinely distinct property, ephemeral private sharing between close friends, is a property a brand cannot use.

## 9. Real cost per week

Fifteen minutes a week to reupload existing vertical video, or two hours a week to make anything native. Neither is worth spending.

Going quiet for a month costs nothing, because there is nothing there to decay.

## 10. Rules and account risk

Religious content is unrestricted. Nothing found that is specific to religious imagery. No elevated account risk identified.

## 11. Is there a Latter-day Saint audience here today

**Thin, and the temple audience is absent.** Verified logged out on 3 September 2026 via `snapchat.com/explore/`, which shows Users, Subscribe profiles with follower counts, Spotlight videos with view and like counts, Lenses and Places.

**Searching "lds temple" returned almost nothing relevant.** The results were Hindu and other temples, an account called "Rejoice Temple", "Dr Bryan Spell Temple" advertising "Restore Your Broken Homes | Manifestations", and "Mr Mateta Temple" at 3.4k followers. The single most important search term for this brand returns noise.

**Searching "lds"** returned Spotlight videos with real numbers: "Ty & Kena Metcalfe's Church Outfit Change Routine" at 86k views and 5.7k likes, "Dawson Caught in Unexpected Doorway Encounter" at 18k, "Awest Shares a Quick Message About Faith and Relationships" at 7.4k. Subscribe profiles were tiny: tkb922 at 432 followers, ldsmira at 832, ldschraderv2 at 17. The largest "lds" match was lestreetie91 at 8.4k followers, a French motorcyclist. A false match.

**Searching "latter day saint"** returned "Mormon Lifestyle Explained: One Husband, One Wife" at 27k views, "Jordyn Addresses Religious Bias Against the Church" at 8.0k, "Latter Day Saints Church Promotes Controversial 'Mormon Boy' T-Shirt" at 4.7k, and "Tithing Explained" at 3.5k. Dedicated profiles: montanaconde19 at 3 followers, ldsflow at 6 followers. Larger results were false matches again, including a French optician at 253k. Places results were Latter-day Saint Employment Services offices.

Three conclusions. A Latter-day Saint audience exists on Snapchat but the dedicated accounts have 3 to 832 followers. The content that does exist is lifestyle, apologetics and explainers aimed at outsiders, with no art, apparel or architecture. And the view counts, 3.5k to 86k, run roughly an order of magnitude below what the same community produces on Instagram, where I observed 74K to 229K in the same subject area on the same day.

## Verdict

Skip. Snapchat does nothing the others do not, its US audience is actively shrinking, it misses half the brand's buyers on age alone, it has no shelf life, and its search returns Hindu temples for the brand's core term. This is the first assessment Snapchat has had and it should also be the last unless something changes materially.
