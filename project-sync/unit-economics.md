Notes:

- Second print location on Tapstitch costs $2.99, which is what made the front logo affordable.
- The sweatshirt blank costs more than the hoodie's ($16.57 vs $14.92). All in, the two are within $0.57, but the sweatshirt sells for $10 less. Either raise its price or accept the gap on purpose. Prices are unchanged for now.
- Each extra item in an order adds shipping. The US per-item rates have not been read yet, and they need to be before any multi-buy offer is built.
- Against a stated $22 to $40 acquisition cost, a single tee does not reliably pay for its customer. AOV is the structural problem. The levers are multi-temple orders, the personalization premium (paused), and hoodie mix.
- Extended sizes: the tee goes to 3XL, fleece stops at 2XL. Size-level cost differences have not been broken out here.
